1. Purpose
Users will be able to utilize the doser to accurately and precisely dispense a specific amount of powdered substance up till the 100 gram limit. This machine is able to safely replicate results with 8 powders: cornstarch, baking soda, ground cinnamon, table salt, granulated sugar, cocoa powder, powdered sugar, and all-purpose flour.


2. Scope
This applies to all users operating PharmaTech Rx® 's Doser.


3. Tools Needed
* M3 wrench and M3 screwdriver (Also known as Allen Key)
* Personal protective equipment: safety goggles (DISCLAIMER: PharmaTech Rx®’s Doser is intended only for the use of the standard 8 powders (cornstarch, baking soda, ground cinnamon, table salt, granulated sugar, cocoa powder, powdered sugar, and all-purpose flour) listed. When utilizing pharmacy grade substances, it is mandatory for users to use full PPE such as powder-free gloves and a gown for non-hazardous powders, and double gloves, a respirator mask such as an N95, safety goggles, a disposable gown, shoe covers, and a hair cover.)


4. Procedure Steps
1. Press the “edit” button on slot 1 on the kiosk.
2. Use the dropdown menu to select the appropriate, desired powder you will be using and press back.
3. Take the powder you will be using and pour it into the hopper through the cutouts on the top of the hopper. Pour from both sides.
4.  Press continue.
5. Next, press the tare (O/T) button in the middle of the scale in order to tare the scale. Press and wait a few seconds and repeat until it reaches 0 grams. 
6. Press the slot with the powder picked previously (in this case slot 1 always), enter the desired number of grams to dispense, and press save. Keep in mind that this doser dispenses a max of 100 grams.
7. Press start. 
8. Watch the auger. If it starts jamming, you see any smoke or smell anything burning, or any other signs that the machine is malfunctioning, press FORCE STOP. 
9. Once the kiosk reads “DONE”, the operation is complete. Check on the scale that it reached the correct quantity within ±3% error.
10. Press the “clear all + return to home” button.
11. Discard any remaining powder from the hopper. 
12. Use the M3 wrench and M3 screwdriver to deassemble the hopper from its lid and wipe the auger, hopper, and its surroundings clean.
5. Expected Outcome
In the end, users will be left with their desired number of grams of their desired powder. 


6. Common Mistakes
A common mistake is to forget to tare the scale. Read the directions carefully above and be sure that after the scale is tared, it is at 0.000 grams.
